Non-Pregnant Training options: Personal Training — One-on-one coaching. A trainer builds workouts around the person’s goals, teaches proper form, provides accountability, and can adjust training for experience level, injuries, or specific fitness targets. GPP (General Physical Preparedness) — A small-group, all-around fitness class. It mixes functional movements, strength work, conditioning, weightlifting, and some gymnastics-style bodyweight exercises. Think: “get stronger, fitter, and more capable in everyday life,” rather than training only for one sport. Their GPP sessions are one hour, capped at six people, and designed to be modified for beginners through experienced athletes. LIFT — A barbell-focused strength class. Members work on movements such as squats, deadlifts, presses, and similar lifts, with coaching on mechanics and technique. It is for someone who wants to build strength, improve form, get more toned, or pursue a heavier personal record safely. SHRED — A high-intensity interval training, or HIIT, workout. It combines cardio, strength, and conditioning in full-body circuits, including rowing machines and Assault air bikes. This is the class for people who want a challenging sweat, calorie burn, endurance work, and full-body conditioning. Boxing — Fitness boxing rather than necessarily competitive boxing. It uses heavy-bag drills, punches such as jabs and crosses, core training, conditioning, and endurance work. It suits beginners who want a fun, stress-relieving workout as well as athletes who want to sharpen punching technique and conditioning. ENDURE — A 40-minute, music-driven cardio circuit. It emphasizes sustained effort and muscle endurance through indoor cycling, rowing, core work, and toning-style movements. This is best described as a high-energy group cardio workout with a strength-endurance element. Yoga (Restorative Zen Flow) — An occasional pop-up recovery and mobility class.
